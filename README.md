# ⚡ LeadLooking

**LeadLooking** runs on autopilot and finds potential leads for your business from Reddit, every single day, automatically.

No more manually scrolling through subreddits. LeadLooking monitors relevant communities, identifies posts where people are looking for solutions you offer, and surfaces them for you daily :)

---

## ✨ Features

- 🤖 **Fully Automated** — Set it up once and let it run. LeadLooking works in the background on a daily schedule with zero manual effort.
- 🎯 **Targeted Lead Discovery** — Scans Reddit posts and comments for buying signals, questions, and pain points that match your product or service.
- 📋 **Daily Lead Reports** — Get a structured summary of fresh leads every day, including the post link, subreddit, and why it's a match.
- 🔧 **Configurable Keywords & Subreddits** — Define exactly which subreddits to watch and what keywords signal a potential lead.
- 🚫 **Duplicate Filtering** — Already-seen posts are tracked so you never see the same lead twice.
